I am currently **Associate Director of the Barcelona Supercomputing Center (BSC)**, one of Europe's leading research institutions in high-performance computing and artificial intelligence. My work focuses on advancing AI research, developing cutting-edge computational infrastructure, and strengthening Europe's technological sovereignty. With over 20 years of experience in AI, computer vision, and research leadership, I am particularly interested in bridging fundamental research, engineering, and real-world impact.

Before joining BSC in 2025, I spent nine years at **Meta**, where I led the Responsible AI organization, overseeing research and engineering efforts to make AI systems safer, more robust, and trustworthy, including the Llama family of generative AI models. Previously, I directed teams working on AI Integrity, computer vision, and adversarial machine learning. Prior to Meta, I was a **Principal Researcher at Microsoft**, working on computer vision and machine learning technologies that contributed to products such as Kinect, HoloLens, and Azure. I hold a PhD from the Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya (UPC) and a Master's degree from EPFL in Switzerland.

Beyond technology, I am also a **pianist and musicologist**, with a longstanding interest in music history and performance. My musicological research has focused particularly on rediscovering the contributions of Catalan composers in the Americas, resulting in several books and historical studies.
